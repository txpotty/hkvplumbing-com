    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Madison

    A sudden water softener breakdown can leave your home vulnerable to instant hard water problems, especially if it leads to a leak. We offer emergency water softener repair services to address urgent issues promptly.     Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Madison

    A softener using far too much salt or water usually has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can significantly cut salt and water use.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Madison

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Madison, WI

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Madison

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener probably needs attention. HKV Plumbing offers comprehensive diagnostic services to identify the root cause of these hard water symptoms. We check water hardness levels, inspect all system components, and provide an accurate assessment and repair plan suited to your home in Madison.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Madison

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again after the next cycle.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Madison, WI

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can effectively break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, frequent in older systems.

    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Madison

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the culprit. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process revitalizes your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.
