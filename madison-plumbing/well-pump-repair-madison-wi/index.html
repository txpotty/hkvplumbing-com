    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Madison, WI

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Sewer backups during major storms are a common problem, making backwater valves and battery backup sump pumps worthwhile. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Madison, WI

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.
