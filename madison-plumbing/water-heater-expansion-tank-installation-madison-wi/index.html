    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Madison

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and wearing out early.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Madison

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is probably seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays stable.
