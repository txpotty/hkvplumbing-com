    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Madison

    Wisconsin has a lot of older housing, and many homes still have cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines or clay sewer laterals. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are typical signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Madison

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Madison

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Madison

    Preventative drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups. We offer routine drain camera inspections to identify grease buildup, roots, or other obstructions before they cause a problem. Our hydrojet plumbing service can clear out stubborn blockages, keeping your drainage system clear and free-flowing, which is especially important for homes with mature trees near the sewer line.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Madison

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Gas Line and Repipe Inspections in Madison, WI

    Safety is critical with gas lines, and our maintenance includes thorough inspections for leaks, corrosion, and proper valve operation.     Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Madison

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Madison, WI

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a detailed estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and peace of mind.
