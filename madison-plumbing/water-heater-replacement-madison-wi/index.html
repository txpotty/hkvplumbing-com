    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Madison

    The cost of water heater replacement in Madison, WI typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of unit (gas, electric, tankless or hybrid), its capacity and any needed plumbing modifications. Our team will provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs and existing setup.

    Upgrading provides more reliable hot water, improved energy efficiency and lower utility bills, better performance and a reduced risk of leaks or breakdowns. Newer models often have helpful features and longer warranties, improving comfort and safety in your home.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the right GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Madison homes.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line adjustments.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. HKV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is protected.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Water softeners are a common feature in many Wisconsin homes, helping protect water heaters, fixtures and appliances from scale. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help extend that life.
