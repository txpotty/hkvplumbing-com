    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Madison

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Madison

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Madison for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is crucial for handling heavy rainfall.
