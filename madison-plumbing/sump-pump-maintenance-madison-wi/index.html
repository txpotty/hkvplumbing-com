    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Madison

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage.     Watch for a pump that runs constantly, hums without moving water, cycles very often or makes grinding noises. Rust, a stuck float or a musty smell around the pit are also warning signs.

    If your basement is finished or storms often knock out your power, a backup is a wise choice. We install and maintain battery and water-powered backup systems.

    Not every home does, but homes with basements, crawlspaces, below-grade garages or low-lying lots often rely on them. Sewer backups during intense storms are a common problem, making backwater valves and battery backup sump pumps worthwhile.

    If your primary sump pump fails during a power outage and you don't have a working battery backup or water-powered backup system, your basement is at risk of flooding. This underscores the importance of maintaining backup systems.

    Key signs include unusual noises like grinding or rattling, the pump running constantly, failing to turn on, visible rust or corrosion, or water pooling in your basement. Don't wait for a flood to call us.
