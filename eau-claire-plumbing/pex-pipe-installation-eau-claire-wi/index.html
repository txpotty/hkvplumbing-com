    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Eau Claire, WI

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Eau Claire

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Eau Claire

    The main water line brings water into your home, and a failing one can cause serious issues. We provide expert PEX main water line installation, replacing old, damaged lines with durable PEX.     Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Eau Claire, WI

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.
