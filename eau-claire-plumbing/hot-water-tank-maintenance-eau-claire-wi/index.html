    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Eau Claire, WI

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years.     Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Eau Claire, WI
