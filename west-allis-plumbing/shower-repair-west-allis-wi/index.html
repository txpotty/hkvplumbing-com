    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in West Allis

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers quickly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will make sure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in West Allis, WI

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure when needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed quickly.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in West Allis, WI

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in adjoining rooms.

    Shower Diverter Repair in West Allis, WI

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Tub and Shower Combination Repair in West Allis, WI

    Many homes in West Allis, WI feature tub and shower combination units, which means their plumbing is connected.
