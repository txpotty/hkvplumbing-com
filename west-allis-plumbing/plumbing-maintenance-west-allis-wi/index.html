    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in West Allis

    Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, and many communities are working to replace them over time. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.

    Emergency Plumbing Readiness in West Allis, WI

    While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still smart.     Water Softener Maintenance in West Allis

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in West Allis, WI

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in West Allis

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.
