    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in West Allis

    The cost of water heater replacement in West Allis, WI usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and needed plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity affect the final price.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    In general, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a sensible option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Hard water is common across much of Wisconsin, especially where supply comes from groundwater moving through limestone and dolomite. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help extend that life.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the appropriate GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom West Allis homes.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. HKV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is covered.

    Yes, in many areas of West Allis and Wisconsin, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement to make sure the installation meets local safety and building codes. HKV Plumbing handles all necessary permitting on your behalf, so you do not have to worry about compliance.
