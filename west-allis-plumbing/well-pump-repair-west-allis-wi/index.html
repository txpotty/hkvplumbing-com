    Pressure Switch Replacement in West Allis

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive typically shows a code that guides diagnosis.     Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in West Allis, WI

    Control and pressure switch repairs usually cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500.     Jet Pump System Repair in West Allis, WI

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. HKV Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in West Allis operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.
