    Most drips come from a worn cartridge or seal. Hard water scale can damage cartridges and cause leaks sooner. Replacing the cartridge usually solves the problem.

    Minor repairs like replacing a faucet or toilet typically do not require a permit.     The lifespan of bathroom plumbing fixtures varies, with toilets lasting 20 to 50 years, faucets 15 to 20 years, and shower valves 20 to 30 years. Hard water is common across much of Wisconsin, especially where supply comes from groundwater moving through limestone and dolomite.     To prevent clogs, avoid flushing anything but toilet paper down the toilet. For sinks and showers, use drain strainers to catch hair and soap scum.
