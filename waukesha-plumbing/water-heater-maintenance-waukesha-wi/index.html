    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Waukesha, WI

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Waukesha

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Waukesha

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit.     For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections.     Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Waukesha, WI

    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Waukesha

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup. We also inspect the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and check for any leaks or corrosion on the tank and connections. This service helps prevent premature failure and improves heating efficiency, keeping your energy bills in check.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Waukesha

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Waukesha, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.
