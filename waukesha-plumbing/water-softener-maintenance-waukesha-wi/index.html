    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Waukesha, WI

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to ensure these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed.     Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Waukesha, WI

    The brine tank is essential for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can build up, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a frequent issue we address in Waukesha homes.

    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Waukesha

    Regular inspections are the cornerstone of effective water softener maintenance. Our technicians will examine your entire system, including the brine tank, resin tank, control valve and all connecting pipes. We check for leaks, corrosion, proper salt levels and any signs of wear that could lead to future problems. This proactive approach helps extend the life of your unit and keeps performance consistent, especially since water hardness varies from place to place.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Waukesha

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Waukesha, WI

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without wasting salt or water.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Waukesha, WI

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can accumulate in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and confirming the float valve operates correctly. This step is crucial for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Waukesha, WI.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Waukesha, WI

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.
