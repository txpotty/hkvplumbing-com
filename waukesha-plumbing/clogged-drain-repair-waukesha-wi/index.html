    The cost of clogged drain repair in Waukesha, WI can vary widely, usually ranging from $150 to $500 for standard clogs cleared with snaking or augering.     To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down kitchen drains, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles, and flush only toilet paper. Regular professional drain maintenance, especially in areas with hard water, can also help limit mineral buildup.

    Hard water is common across much of Wisconsin, especially where supply comes from groundwater moving through limestone and dolomite. Hard water leaves mineral scale that narrows pipe walls and gives grease and hair something to cling to. Older homes may also have cast iron pipes that rust and roughen inside. Both conditions make clogs form faster.

    You should call a professional if a plunger or simple home remedies don't work, if you experience multiple clogged drains, sewage smells, or water backing up. These symptoms often point to a deeper, more serious issue in your main sewer line that requires expert diagnosis and specialized tools.

    A plunger or a hand-held hair snake can handle many simple tub and sink clogs. If the clog returns, affects more than one fixture or does not respond to a plunger, call a plumber. Forcing a cable can crack old pipes or scratch fixtures.
