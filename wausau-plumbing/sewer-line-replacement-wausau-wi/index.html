    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Wausau, WI

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Wausau, WI while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Wausau

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is installed and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a smart option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you honestly if your line is a candidate.
