    Detecting Pipe Damage and Corrosion in Wausau, WI

    Sewer lines can suffer from various forms of damage, including cracks, breaks, corrosion and sagging sections due to shifting soil. Our camera inspections provide a detailed visual assessment of the pipe's interior, identifying these structural issues. We can determine the severity and exact location of damage, helping you decide between spot repair, relining or full pipe replacement. Early detection is key to preventing catastrophic failures.

    Root Intrusion Detection in Wausau, WI

    Tree roots are a common problem for sewer lines, especially with the mature trees found in many Wausau neighborhoods. Roots seek out moisture and can infiltrate even tiny cracks in pipes, growing and eventually causing severe blockages or pipe damage. Our high-resolution cameras clearly show root masses, allowing us to recommend appropriate solutions like hydro-jetting or pipe repair. We use professional locators to mark the exact spot above ground.

    Video Camera Inspection for Blockages in Wausau

    When drains slow down or stop completely, a blockage is often the culprit. Our sewer camera inspection can pinpoint exactly where the blockage is located and what's causing it, whether it's grease buildup, foreign objects or sediment. This precision allows us to perform targeted clearing without unnecessary digging, saving you time and money. We can work through bends and junctions to get a full view of the pipe's interior.

    Sewer Scope for Slow Drains in Wausau

    If you are experiencing consistently slow drains or recurring clogs, a sewer scope inspection can pinpoint the exact cause. Instead of guessing, our camera can show whether the issue is a simple blockage, a pipe belly or more serious damage like a collapse. This precise diagnosis in Wausau allows us to recommend the most effective and least invasive repair, saving you from unnecessary digging or repeated efforts.
