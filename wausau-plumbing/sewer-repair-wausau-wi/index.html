    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Wausau, WI

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Freeze and thaw cycles move the ground each year, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Key signs you need sewer repair include multiple slow drains or frequent backups, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, foul sewer odors in or around your home, soggy spots in your yard or an unexplained increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's wise to call for an inspection.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method of repairing or replacing sewer lines with minimal digging, often requiring only one or two access points. It involves techniques like pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, or pipe lining, where a resin-impregnated liner is inserted and cured inside the existing pipe. This protects your landscaping and is less disruptive than traditional methods.

    Watch for multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling toilets, sewage smells in the yard and wet or unusually green patches over the line. Water backing up into a tub or shower when you run the washing machine is another common warning sign.
