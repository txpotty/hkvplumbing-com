    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Wausau, WI

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Hard water is common across much of Wisconsin, especially where supply comes from groundwater moving through limestone and dolomite. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Wausau, WI, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Wausau, WI

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can ruin a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Wausau

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Wausau home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Wausau

    A standard flush and inspection usually runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement typically adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    Common signs include reduced hot water supply, strange noises like rumbling or popping, rust-colored water, a sulfur or rotten egg smell, or water leaking from the tank. These point to issues that maintenance can often resolve.

    In mineral-heavy water a magnesium rod may last three to five years. We measure it at every visit so you replace it before the tank lining starts to rust.
