    Water Heater Maintenance in Wausau

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements.     Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Wausau, WI

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Wausau

    Wisconsin has a lot of older housing, and many homes still have cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines or clay sewer laterals.
