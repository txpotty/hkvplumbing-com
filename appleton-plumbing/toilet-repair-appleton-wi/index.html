    Running Toilet Repair in Appleton

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills. This common issue is usually caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers quickly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Appleton, WI

    Water around the base typically means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a fresh seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Appleton, WI

    Stubborn toilet clogs are a common nuisance in Appleton, WI homes, often caused by non-flushable items or excessive toilet paper. While a plunger can clear minor blockages, persistent clogs indicate a deeper issue in the trap, drain line, or even the main sewer line. Our plumbers utilize professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the toughest clogs without damaging your toilet or pipes. We also inspect for potential causes like root intrusion or pipe corrosion, which are sometimes seen in older homes.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Appleton, WI

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line during every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Appleton

    Cracks in a toilet tank or bowl in Appleton can lead to serious water leakage and require immediate attention. While small hairline cracks might be temporarily sealed, larger or structural cracks often necessitate replacement of the affected component. Our technicians assess the severity of the crack and advise on the most cost-effective and durable solution. We can perform precise tank or bowl replacements, ensuring a watertight seal and proper function, preventing further water damage to your flooring or subfloor.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Appleton, WI

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush might mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Appleton

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.
