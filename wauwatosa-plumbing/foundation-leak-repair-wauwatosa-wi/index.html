    Water Line Reroute in Wauwatosa

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Wauwatosa, WI

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Wauwatosa, WI

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Wauwatosa, WI

    In some cases, the most effective way to repair a foundation leak is by tunneling beneath the slab. This method allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe directly without breaking through your home's interior flooring. HKV Plumbing is experienced in safe and efficient tunneling, limiting disruption to your Wauwatosa, WI home and delivering a complete repair of the damaged pipe section.

    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Wauwatosa

    Many foundation leaks start from exterior factors like poor drainage, overflowing gutters, or saturated soil. We assess your home's exterior to identify these culprits. Our solutions include grading adjustments, French drain installation, and downspout extensions to redirect water away from your foundation. Proper exterior water management is key for preventing hydrostatic pressure buildup against your foundation walls in Wauwatosa and keeping water out.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Wauwatosa

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs requires care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When the risk is too great, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.
