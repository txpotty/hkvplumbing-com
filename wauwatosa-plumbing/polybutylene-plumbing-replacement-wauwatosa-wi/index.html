    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Wauwatosa

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Hard water is common across much of Wisconsin, especially where supply comes from groundwater moving through limestone and dolomite. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened.
