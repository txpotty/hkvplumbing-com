    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Green Bay, WI

    Modern WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and dual-flush models offer an even lighter option for liquid waste. Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet makes a noticeable difference on your water bill. Some local water providers have offered rebates for high-efficiency toilets, so it is worth checking with yours. We help you choose a model with a strong MaP flush rating.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Green Bay

    Pressure-assisted toilets provide a strong flush, ideal for commercial settings or homes with specific needs. Their installation differs from gravity-fed models, requiring careful attention to the pressure tank and water supply. We make sure all components are correctly fitted and calibrated for best performance. Our plumbers in Green Bay, WI are skilled in setting up these robust systems to prevent water hammer or excessive noise.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Installation in Green Bay

    Smart toilets and bidet seats from TOTO Washlet, Kohler and Bio Bidet need a water connection and usually a nearby GFCI outlet. We install the T-valve or dedicated supply, mount the seat and test spray, heat and dryer functions. If an outlet is needed, we coordinate with a licensed electrician. We also explain basic maintenance like cleaning the nozzle and filter.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Green Bay, WI

    Upgrade to a high-efficiency toilet (HET) to save water and reduce your utility bills, a smart investment for any home.     Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Green Bay, WI

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning simple, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is easiest during a remodel when the wall is open.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Green Bay

    In new homes and additions, we set toilets after the finished floor is in so the flange sits at the right height. We verify the drain is correctly vented and the rough-in matches the fixture you selected. Each toilet gets a new wax or wax-free seal, a stainless supply line and a quarter-turn angle stop. We flush-test and check for leaks before final inspection.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Green Bay, WI

    Remodels often raise the floor with new tile, which can leave the old flange too low. We use flange extenders or replace the flange so the seal compresses correctly. If you are moving the toilet, we can relocate the drain, which on a slab foundation requires cutting and patching concrete. We plan timing so the toilet goes in after tile and grout have cured.
