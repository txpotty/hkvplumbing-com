    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Green Bay

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Green Bay. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are notorious for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers ensure proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Green Bay

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Green Bay, WI

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Green Bay

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.
