    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Green Bay

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and common code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    Many households of two to three people do well with a 40 gallon tank, while larger families often need 50 gallons or more. We size according to your bathroom count and usage.

    HKV Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that fits your budget and hot water demands.

    Manufacturer tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the right GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Green Bay homes.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. HKV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.
