    Copper to PEX Conversions in Green Bay

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Hard water is common across much of Wisconsin, especially where supply comes from groundwater moving through limestone and dolomite. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Green Bay, WI

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Green Bay, WI

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.
