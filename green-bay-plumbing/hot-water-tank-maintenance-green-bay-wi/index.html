    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Green Bay, WI

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it affects the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Green Bay, WI

    A annual flush costs a small fraction of a new tank, and a new installed tank often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more. We give you an honest read on whether maintenance is still worth it. If a tank is past 10 to 12 years old, shows rust at the base or leaks from the seams, we will tell you replacement is the smarter spend. When the tank still has life, maintenance keeps it running well for years.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Green Bay

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Green Bay home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Green Bay, WI

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Commercial and Rental Tank Maintenance in Green Bay

    Property managers, restaurants and small offices often run several tanks that work much harder than a home unit. We set up scheduled maintenance for landlords and businesses, flushing each tank and logging the anode condition and valve test date. Records like these help with insurance and with planning replacements before a tank fails during business hours. We maintain 50 to 100 gallon commercial models as well as residential tanks in rental units.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Green Bay, WI

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank absorbs excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is key to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and maintaining system integrity.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Green Bay, WI

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more evenly and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Green Bay, WI, where mineral content in the water is a frequent concern.
