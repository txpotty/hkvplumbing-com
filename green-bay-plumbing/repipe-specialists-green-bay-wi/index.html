    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Green Bay, WI

    Hard water is common across much of Wisconsin, especially where supply comes from groundwater moving through limestone and dolomite. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Green Bay, WI

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. HKV Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Green Bay, WI, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also significantly dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures.
