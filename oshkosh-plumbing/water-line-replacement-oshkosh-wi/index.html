    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Oshkosh

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and discolored water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often noticeable right away.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Oshkosh

    The main water line is the primary pipe delivering water from the municipal supply to your home. When this critical line fails due to age, shifting soil, or severe leaks, a full replacement is often the most cost-effective long-term solution. We carefully excavate, remove the old line, and install new, durable piping, ensuring proper connection to your home's plumbing system and the city's main.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Oshkosh, WI

    For homeowners seeking to minimize disruption to their landscaping, trenchless water line replacement offers an effective alternative.     PEX Water Line Installation in Oshkosh, WI

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and very popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it highly durable for most water conditions. Frozen pipes are a regular winter risk in Wisconsin, and deep frost can even reach water service lines buried underground. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a significant advantage when a cold snap hits Oshkosh. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and quicker installation.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Oshkosh, WI

    Type K copper is a durable option for underground water lines. It resists punctures and lasts for decades. Copper costs more than PEX but is favored by some homeowners. We install copper with proper bedding to protect it. We can also install sleeves where it passes through foundations.
