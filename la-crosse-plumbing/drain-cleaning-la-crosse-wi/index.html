    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in La Crosse

    Most single fixture drain cleanings in La Crosse run about $125 to $275. Main line cleaning from a cleanout typically costs $175 to $450, and adding a camera inspection usually adds $150 to $350.

    Hydro jetting is best for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    Most standard drain cleaning for a single fixture can be done within 1 to 2 hours. More extensive jobs, such as main sewer line clearing or hydro jetting, may take 3 to 4 hours or longer, depending on the complexity of the clog.

    For most homes, an annual or twice-yearly professional drain cleaning is sufficient, especially for kitchen and main lines. If you have frequent clogs or an older plumbing system, more frequent cleanings might be helpful to prevent major issues.

    Tree roots are drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines. During dry periods, roots will seek out any tiny crack or loose joint in older pipes to reach water, then grow and expand, causing serious blockages. This is a common issue for homes with mature trees.

    Caustic and acid cleaners can damage older cast iron, soften plastic joints and harm the person using them. They also seldom clear the full clog. Mechanical cleaning is the safer choice and more effective.

    We usually advise against chemical drain cleaners. While they might offer a temporary fix, they can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are often useless against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease buildup. They can also pose health risks.

    Usually, you do not need a permit for routine drain cleaning or unclogging a drain. Permits are generally required for more extensive plumbing work, such as replacing a sewer line or making significant changes to your plumbing system.
