    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in La Crosse, WI

    Sewer lines in La Crosse, WI can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Freeze and thaw cycles move the ground each year, which can crack older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in La Crosse

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in La Crosse that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Root Intrusion Repair in La Crosse, WI

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in La Crosse

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.
