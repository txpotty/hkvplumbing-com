    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in La Crosse

    Residential storm drain cleaning in La Crosse usually runs $200 to $500 depending on the number of inlets and pipe length. Dry well cleaning and commercial systems typically cost more and are quoted after a site visit.

    We guarantee that the lines we clean will flow when we leave, and we back our workmanship in writing. Crushed or broken pipe is identified and quoted separately.

    Most homes take 1 to 3 hours. Large commercial sites or dry wells may take a half day or more.

    Common signs include water pooling around your property after rain, slow drainage from downspouts, visible debris piling up at catch basin grates, and unusual odors coming from the drains. If you notice any of these, it is a good idea to have your storm drains checked.

    Just before your area's rainy or storm season starts is best. A second check after the season ends is smart for properties with lots of trees or gravel.

    While you can remove surface debris from drain grates, deeply clogged storm drains need professional tools like hydro jetters and camera equipment for effective cleaning. DIY attempts can sometimes push debris further down or damage the pipes, leading to more costly repairs. It is safer and more reliable to hire a professional.

    It is generally recommended to have your storm drains inspected and cleaned at least once a year. Properties with many trees or those that see frequent heavy rains may need twice-yearly cleaning to prevent buildup and blockages.

    Cleaning does not need a permit. New dry wells or major drainage changes may require permits or registration, and we can help you.
