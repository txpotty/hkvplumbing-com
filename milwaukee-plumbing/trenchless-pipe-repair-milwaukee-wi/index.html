    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Milwaukee, WI

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is excellent for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. HKV Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Milwaukee, WI.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Milwaukee

    Commercial properties often have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Trenchless repair avoids costly concrete demolition and disruption to tenants and customers. We line larger diameter pipes and multiple laterals. We schedule work to limit impact on business operations.

    Pipe Bursting in Milwaukee, WI

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.
