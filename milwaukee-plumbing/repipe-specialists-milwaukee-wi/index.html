    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Milwaukee

    Most PEX repipes for Wisconsin homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper repipes typically run 20 to 40 percent more.

    Copper is a rigid, durable metal with a long lifespan, known for its resistance to bacteria. PEX is a flexible plastic tubing that is more affordable, easier to install, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and very quiet. Both are excellent choices for repiping, and our specialists can help you decide which material is best suited for your home and budget in Milwaukee, WI.

    Most whole-home repipes can be completed within 3 to 5 days, depending on the size and complexity of your home and the chosen piping material. We strive to minimize water shut-off periods, often restoring water service by the end of each workday. Our team works efficiently to get your plumbing fully operational as soon as possible.

    HKV Plumbing stands behind our repiping work with solid warranties covering both labor and materials. The specific terms of your warranty will be provided in your detailed estimate. We use high-quality materials from trusted brands like Uponor PEX and Mueller Streamline copper, ensuring long-term reliability for your new plumbing system in Milwaukee, WI.

    Repiping is a complex plumbing project that requires specialized tools, knowledge of plumbing codes, and significant experience. Attempting a DIY repipe can lead to costly mistakes, leaks, and potential code violations. It's strongly recommended to hire professional repipe specialists like HKV Plumbing to make sure the job is done correctly, safely, and to code.

    PEX resists scale and pinhole corrosion and handles expansion well, which makes it a strong choice for hard water. Copper is durable too but costs more and can pinhole in corrosive water.

    Common signs include discolored or rusty water, low water pressure at multiple fixtures, frequent leaks or pinhole leaks, and strange noises coming from your pipes. If your home has galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes, a repipe is often a preventive and wise investment to prevent future problems.

    Both PEX and copper are strong choices for repiping in Wisconsin, each with distinct advantages.
