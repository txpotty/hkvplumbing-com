    New Sump Pit Installation in Milwaukee

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Milwaukee, WI

    Strong storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that warn you about problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Milwaukee, WI

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Milwaukee

    The cost of sump pump installation in Milwaukee, WI usually ranges from $600 to $1,800, depending on the pump type, whether a new pit needs to be dug and the complexity of the plumbing and electrical work. Battery backup systems or advanced features will raise the price. We provide a detailed quote after assessing your home.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods may fail sooner, so periodic testing is important.

    Most manufacturers offer warranties from 3 to 5 years, and some premium models carry longer coverage. We also stand behind our installation workmanship.

    HKV Plumbing installs a variety of reputable sump pump brands known for their durability and performance. Our common selections include Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty Pumps. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your specific needs and budget for your Milwaukee home.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, ideal for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    A well-maintained sump pump usually lasts between 7 and 10 years. The lifespan can vary depending on the quality of the pump, how often it runs and how well it is maintained. Regular inspections and cleaning can help extend its life.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, if a new sump pit needs to be excavated or if there are complex plumbing or electrical adjustments, the process might take a full day. We always aim for efficiency without compromising quality.
