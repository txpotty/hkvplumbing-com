    Freeze and Heat Protection for Backflow Preventers in Milwaukee, WI

    Above-ground backflow assemblies are exposed to summer sun and winter cold. Insulating pipes on exterior walls, shutting off outdoor faucets and keeping garages warm help prevent most freeze damage. Freezing can crack bodies and bonnets, while UV exposure breaks down plastic parts. We install insulated pouches, heated enclosures or lockable cages as needed. Protecting the assembly helps it pass testing and last for years.
