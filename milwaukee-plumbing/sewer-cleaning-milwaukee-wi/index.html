    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Milwaukee, WI

    Cleaning removes what is in the pipe, but it cannot fix a crack, a belly or a collapsed section. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera will often show why. We compare the yearly cost of maintenance cleanings to a spot repair, lining or replacement. Sometimes regular cleaning is the smart move for a few years. Other times a repair now saves more over time, and we show you the math.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Milwaukee

    Some homes have no accessible cleanout, which forces techs to pull a toilet or go through a roof vent.     Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Milwaukee

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. Cleaning keeps you running while you budget for a replacement.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Milwaukee

    For the most stubborn sewer line clogs, hydro jetting is the gold standard. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and even tree roots from the interior walls of your sewer pipes. HKV Plumbing employs professional hydro jetting equipment for sewer cleaning in Milwaukee, delivering a thorough cleaning that leaves your pipes virtually spotless, greatly reducing the chance of future blockages and extending the life of your sewer system.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Milwaukee

    Trees and shrubs send roots toward any moisture they can find, and a sewer joint is a steady source during dry months. Thirsty yard trees and shrubs are common causes in local yards. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out. Heavy root growth may need a follow-up with a jetter to clear fine root hairs. A camera inspection then shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining or repair.
