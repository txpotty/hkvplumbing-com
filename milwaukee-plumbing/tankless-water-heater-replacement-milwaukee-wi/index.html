    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Milwaukee, WI

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. During extreme cold, some communities ask residents to run a small stream of water to keep service lines from freezing. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Milwaukee, WI

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units usually need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We evaluate your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Plan for a conversion to take a full day.

    Tankless vs. Tank Water Heater Conversion in Milwaukee, WI

    Thinking about converting from a traditional tank water heater to a tankless system in your Milwaukee, WI home? This is a common and beneficial upgrade, but it requires specialized expertise. We handle the entire conversion, which can involve rerouting gas lines, upgrading electrical service, modifying water lines and installing new venting. Our team ensures a smooth transition, so you can enjoy the long-term benefits of a modern, efficient tankless system.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Milwaukee
