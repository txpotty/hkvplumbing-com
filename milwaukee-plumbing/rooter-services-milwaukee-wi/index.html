    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Milwaukee

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Our technicians take every precaution to keep your home clean during rooter service. We use drop cloths and protective gear to contain any mess, so the work area is tidy after the job is done. We respect your property and aim for a clean, efficient service.

    No permit is required for routine rooting. Installing a new cleanout or repairing pipe may require one. We handle permits for any repair work.

    Tree roots are naturally drawn to water and nutrients, which they find in sewer lines through tiny cracks or loose joints. As roots grow, they can expand and completely block the pipe, a frequent issue in Milwaukee neighborhoods with mature trees and older pipes.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a permanent fix.

    While chemical drain cleaners are available, HKV Plumbing generally advises against them. They often only partially clear clogs, can damage pipes over time, and carry safety risks. Professional rooter services are much more effective, safer for your plumbing, and provide a lasting solution to blockages.

    Slow drains in several fixtures, gurgling toilets and sewage odors are typical signs. Water backing up into a tub when you flush is another clue. Frequent clogs in the same line also point to a deeper blockage.

    Rooting is safe when done by trained technicians using the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. We watch for warning signs and stop if the pipe shows damage.
