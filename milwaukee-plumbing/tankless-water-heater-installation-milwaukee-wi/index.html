    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Milwaukee, WI

    Tankless installation costs more upfront than a standard tank because of the unit price, gas line upgrades and venting changes.     Tank to Tankless Conversions in Milwaukee

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line often needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and dispose of your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Milwaukee, WI

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Water softeners are a common feature in many Wisconsin homes, helping protect water heaters, fixtures and appliances from scale. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.

    A standard tankless water heater installation, replacing an existing tank, typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If extensive plumbing or gas line changes are required, it may take a full day. Our team works efficiently to restore your hot water as soon as possible.

    While it may seem appealing, tankless water heater installation is complex and should only be performed by a licensed professional. Incorrect installation can be dangerous and cancel warranties.

    In Wisconsin, plumbing and often gas or electrical permits are usually required for tankless water heater installation. HKV Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and makes sure your installation passes all required inspections by local authorities.
