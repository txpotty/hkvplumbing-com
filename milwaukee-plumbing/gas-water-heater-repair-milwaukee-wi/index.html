    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Milwaukee

    Most gas water heater repairs cost $150 to $700. Thermocouple and pilot work is at the low end, while gas control valves and power vent components are at the higher end. We quote before any work begins.

    Look out for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these warrant a professional inspection.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't detect heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a leading reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a vital safety component.

    Most common gas water heater repairs, such as replacing a thermocouple or thermostat, can be finished within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues or those requiring special parts might take longer, but we always aim for same-day resolution whenever possible.

    Typical causes include a weak thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube, a clogged flame arrestor or poor combustion air. Drafts in the garage can also snuff out older pilots.

    On average, a well-maintained gas water heater can last between 10 to 12 years. Regular maintenance, including flushing the tank, can help extend its lifespan. If your unit is nearing this age and having frequent issues, replacement might be more sensible than continuous repairs.
