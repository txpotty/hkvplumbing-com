    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Sheboygan

    That constant drip, drip, drip isn't just annoying, it's a sign of a deeper problem. Most dripping faucets are caused by internal component wear, like a deteriorated rubber washer or a cracked cartridge. Our team provides precise solutions, replacing the specific parts that are failing. We work with all types of faucets, including compression, cartridge, disc, and ball varieties, restoring a quiet and drip-free operation once again.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Sheboygan, WI

    Beyond standard kitchen and bathroom fixtures, we handle repairs for specialty faucets such as pot fillers, utility sink faucets, and outdoor spigots found on many homes.     Cartridge Faucet Repair in Sheboygan, WI

    Many modern single-handle faucets, commonly found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Faucet Handle Repair & Replacement in Sheboygan, WI

    A loose, broken, or stripped faucet handle makes it hard to control water flow and temperature. Over time, daily use can wear down the internal mechanisms or cause the handle itself to break. We offer skilled repair for various handle types, from single-handle mixers to separate hot and cold handles. If a repair isn't feasible, we can recommend and install a suitable replacement handle that matches your existing fixture and style, adding to its lifespan.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Sheboygan

    Weak or sputtering flow is often caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-home water pressure.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Sheboygan, WI

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. However, if the faucet body is cracked, parts are discontinued or it has needed several repairs, replacement might be better value. We give you both prices so you can choose. Many brands like Moen and Delta offer lifetime warranties on cartridges, which can lower your repair cost.

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. During extreme cold, some communities ask residents to run a small stream of water to keep service lines from freezing.
