    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Sheboygan, WI

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff. We service these smaller units, clear their screens and confirm the discharge reaches daylight or an approved drain. During extreme cold, some communities ask residents to run a small stream of water to keep service lines from freezing.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Sheboygan

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Sheboygan residence.

    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Sheboygan

    Over time, sump pits can accumulate dirt, gravel and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch. Our maintenance process includes carefully cleaning the sump pit and removing any obstructions. This lets the pump operate freely and efficiently, avoiding breakdowns and extending its lifespan for your Sheboygan property.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Sheboygan, WI

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Sheboygan, WI.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Sheboygan, WI

    A power outage during a heavy storm can render your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes thorough testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and confirm the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Sheboygan, WI where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Sheboygan

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Sheboygan, WI

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Sheboygan, WI

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.
