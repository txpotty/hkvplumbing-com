    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Sheboygan, WI

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Sheboygan, WI

    For specific pipe leaks, PEX can be used to repair or replace sections of damaged piping. Our plumbers can isolate the damaged section and tie in PEX, providing a durable and flexible fix that holds up to future pressures. This targeted approach is cost-effective for localized issues and brings the benefits of PEX to problem areas of your existing plumbing in Sheboygan, WI.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Sheboygan

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Sheboygan

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Sheboygan can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Sheboygan

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in often run $800 to $2,500.

    A full home PEX repipe for an average-sized home in Sheboygan usually takes 3 to 5 days. Smaller projects or single-line replacements can often be completed in a single day. We aim to minimize disruption and communicate timelines clearly.

    We primarily use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in Wisconsin plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has far fewer joints. Copper is very durable but prone to pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    Multiple pinhole leaks, discolored water, low pressure throughout the house and gray polybutylene pipe are common signs. Recurring slab leaks are also a good reason to repipe overhead.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is highly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are crucial for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not develop pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Sheboygan homes. It also reduces water hammer noise.
