    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Sheboygan, WI

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing severely damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is well suited for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. HKV Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Sheboygan, WI.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Sheboygan, WI

    Main water lines can also be replaced with trenchless methods. We can pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through the old path using bursting or a directional boring tool. This avoids digging across lawns, driveways and walkways. The new line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Sheboygan

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Pipe Bursting in Sheboygan, WI

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints.
