    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Sheboygan

    The cost of grease trap installation in Sheboygan, WI varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation. For a small under-sink hydro-mechanical unit, plan on between $1,500 and $4,000. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors can range from $7,000 to $20,000 or more, including excavation and concrete work.

    The lifespan of a grease trap depends on its type, material, and how well it's maintained. Steel grease traps may last 10 to 15 years, while durable fiberglass or polyethylene units can last 20 to 30 years or even longer. Regular cleaning and proper usage are key to extending its lifespan.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are typically rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.

    Installation time depends on the size and complexity of the grease trap. A small under-sink unit might take a few hours, while a large outdoor interceptor could require several days due to excavation and concrete work. We strive to complete installations efficiently to minimize disruption to your business operations.

    Most food service businesses that cook, wash dishes or prepare food with fats, oils or grease are required to have one. Local pretreatment programs set the specific rules, and we can help you find out what applies to your site.

    Manufacturer warranties vary, with many polyethylene units carrying long structural warranties. We also guarantee our installation workmanship and connections.

    While the terms are often used interchangeably, 'grease trap' typically refers to smaller, indoor hydro-mechanical units, often found under sinks. 'Grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor gravity-fed units with higher capacities. Both serve the same purpose of separating FOG from wastewater.
