    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Sheboygan, WI

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also suggest salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Sheboygan

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly.
