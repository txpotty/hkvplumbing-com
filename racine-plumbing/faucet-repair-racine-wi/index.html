    Compression Faucet Repair in Racine

    Compression faucets, often found in older Racine homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's typically due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks for good. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Racine

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Insulating pipes on exterior walls, shutting off outdoor faucets and keeping garages warm help prevent most freeze damage. We repair leaking packing nuts, replace worn washers and install new frost-free hose bibs when needed. We also add vacuum breakers to meet backflow prevention requirements. A leaking hose bib can waste a lot of water over a long season of yard use.

    Kitchen Faucet Repair in Racine

    Kitchen faucets endure heavy daily use and are prone to specific issues like sprayer malfunctions, clogged aerators, or swivel problems. Our plumbers are skilled at repairing all kitchen faucet styles, including pull-down, pull-out, and commercial-style fixtures.     Cartridge Faucet Repair in Racine, WI

    Many modern single-handle faucets, commonly found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Racine

    Weak or sputtering flow is usually caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-home water pressure.

    Delta & Moen Faucet Repair in Racine, WI

    We have extensive experience repairing and servicing faucets from all leading brands, including popular names like Delta, Moen, Kohler, Pfister, and American Standard.     Dripping Faucet Repair in Racine

We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from building up on your water bill.
