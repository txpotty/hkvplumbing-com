    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Racine

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is feasible or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more economical and reliable option. Our technicians in Racine provide an honest assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Racine, WI

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are frequent causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry. We set the switch to suit your new tank's pre-charge.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Racine

    Many older systems have a tank that is too small for the pump, so the motor starts every time someone washes their hands. Tank size is based on drawdown, which is the available water stored between cut-out and cut-in pressure. For example, a 10 gallon per minute pump usually needs at least 10 gallons of drawdown for a one minute run time. That often means an 80 to 119 gallon tank rather than the 20 gallon unit that came with the home. Upsizing is one of the best ways to extend pump life.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Racine

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A simple test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and verify the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Racine, WI

    Diaphragm tanks like the Amtrol Well-X-Trol use a fixed membrane that separates air above from water below. They are durable, but the steel shell can rust through at the base after years of condensation in a hot, damp equipment space. We replace corroded diaphragm tanks and check the stand and fittings under them. The new tank is leveled and supported so it does not stress the piping. This restores even pressure to every faucet.
