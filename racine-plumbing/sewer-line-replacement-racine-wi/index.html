    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Racine

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in Racine, WI

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods greatly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Racine, WI while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Racine

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Racine, WI

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we plan for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we close it up.
