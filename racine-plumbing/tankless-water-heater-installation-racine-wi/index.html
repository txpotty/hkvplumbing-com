    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Racine, WI

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Water softeners are a standard feature in many Wisconsin homes, helping protect water heaters, fixtures and appliances from scale. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
