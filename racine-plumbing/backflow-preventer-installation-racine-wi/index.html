    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Racine

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Racine. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HKV Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Racine, WI

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.
