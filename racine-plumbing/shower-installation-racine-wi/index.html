    Curbless Shower Installation in Racine

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproofing.

    Shower Pan Installation in Racine

    A correctly installed shower pan is fundamental for preventing leaks and protecting your bathroom subfloor. HKV Plumbing expertly installs various shower pan types, including pre-fabricated units and custom-tiled pans with proper waterproofing membranes. We make sure the pan is level and correctly sloped toward the drain, a vital step that prevents expensive water damage in the long run. We use high-quality sealants for lasting protection.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Racine

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Racine, WI

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach of the seat. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe more safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Racine, WI

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are affordable and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and guest houses. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or crack.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Racine

    Rain showerheads, body sprays and dual heads need careful planning for valve capacity and water pressure. We install thermostatic valves with volume controls and diverters to run multiple outlets. We also confirm your water heater can handle higher flow. The result is a spa-like shower that works as intended.
