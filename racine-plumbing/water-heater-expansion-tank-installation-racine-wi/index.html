    Expansion Tank Replacement in Racine, WI

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a clear sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installing.

    Expansion Tanks With Tankless Water Heaters in Racine, WI

    Tankless water heaters hold very little water, so thermal expansion is minimal in most homes. However, homes with recirculation loops, storage buffer tanks or certain closed system setups may still benefit from an expansion tank. We evaluate your system and only recommend a tank when it serves a genuine purpose. This keeps you from paying for parts you do not need.
