    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Kenosha, WI

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Hard water is common across much of Wisconsin, especially where supply comes from groundwater moving through limestone and dolomite. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Kenosha

    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are skilled in repairing both. PEX offers flexibility and corrosion resistance, while copper is known for its durability. We carry a full stock of appropriate fittings and tools for each material, ensuring a proper, code-compliant repair. Our expertise extends to identifying common failure points for each type, such as fitting issues with PEX or pinhole leaks in older copper lines, frequent in homes with harder water.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Kenosha

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Kenosha

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. Newer homes in Kenosha usually use PEX or copper supply lines, which handle the climate well when properly protected. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Kenosha is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Kenosha

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with minimal disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than conventional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Kenosha looking to protect their property. We'll assess whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Kenosha

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.
