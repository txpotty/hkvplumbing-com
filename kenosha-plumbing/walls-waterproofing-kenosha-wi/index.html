    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Kenosha, WI

    Sump pumps are a key part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during flash floods. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed properly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Drainage System Integration in Kenosha, WI

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Kenosha, WI properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Kenosha, WI

    Waterproofing a shower or wall costs much less than repeated drywall, cabinet and flooring repairs. Ignoring moisture also invites mold, which can be expensive to remediate. A typical shower wall waterproofing project falls in the low thousands, while exterior block sealing varies with wall size. We give you a written quote so you can compare options clearly.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Kenosha

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Kenosha, WI

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Kenosha, WI

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades head off costly drywall repairs.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Kenosha, WI

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Kenosha

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area walls.
