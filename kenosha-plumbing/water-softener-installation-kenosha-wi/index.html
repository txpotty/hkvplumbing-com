    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Kenosha

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps working well.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Kenosha

    Modern water softeners offer improved efficiency, using less salt and water during the regeneration process. We install a range of high-efficiency models that can lead to real savings on your utility bills.     Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Kenosha

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems condition the water to prevent scale buildup without removing beneficial minerals or adding salt. While not true softeners, they are an effective option for scale prevention and are popular in many Kenosha households.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Kenosha

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Kenosha home. This means every faucet, shower and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including necessary bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide comprehensive protection against hard water throughout your property.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Kenosha, WI

    Municipal water often contains chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter paired with a softener removes those chemicals and protects resin.
