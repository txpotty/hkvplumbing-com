    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Kenosha

    Residential hydro jetting in Kenosha usually costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    Hydro jetting is very effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often leaves behind.

    HKV Plumbing uses professional-grade hydro jetting equipment from trusted manufacturers like Spartan Tool and MyTana. These brands are known for their reliability, powerful performance, and safety features, so we can deliver the most thorough cleaning for your pipes.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection shows damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    While hydro jetting effectively removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can discuss during our assessment.

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or caved in.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and wash debris away.

    While small, low-pressure drain jetters are available for rent, professional hydro jetting equipment is far more powerful and requires training to operate safely and effectively. Improper use can damage pipes, cause injury, or fail to clear the blockage. For severe clogs, it is always best to call experienced professionals like HKV Plumbing.
