    Lateral and Spot Lining in Kenosha

    Not every pipe needs to be lined end to end. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, we can install a short sectional liner. This costs less than a full-length liner and is completed fast. We verify the rest of the line is sound before recommending a spot liner.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Kenosha, WI

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is well suited for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. HKV Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Kenosha, WI.
