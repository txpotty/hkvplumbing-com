    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Janesville, WI

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak.     Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Janesville, WI

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Janesville, WI

    Basement wall sealing is a critical part of walls waterproofing. Boilers, furnaces and water heaters work hard through the winter, so seasonal maintenance helps prevent failures during deep cold. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier, healthier environment in your Janesville, WI basement.
