    Drop-In Sink Installation in Janesville, WI

    Drop-in or self-rimming sinks are the most forgiving style and a good choice for laminate counters. We check the cutout, seal the rim with silicone or plumber's putty as the maker specifies, and connect a new pop-up drain. This is often the fastest and most affordable sink installation. It is also a simple way to refresh an older vanity.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Janesville, WI

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter, which changes the drain height and faucet requirements. We install the mounting ring, set the bowl level, and use a vessel-specific drain with or without an overflow as the basin requires. We also confirm your faucet is a tall or wall-mount model that reaches past the rim. The trap arm is adjusted so everything lines up under the counter.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Janesville

    Undermount sinks offer a sleek look and are popular for their easy-to-clean design. Their installation requires precise cutting of countertops and secure mounting from underneath. Our plumbers are experts in creating watertight seals and using appropriate clips and adhesives to ensure the sink is firmly in place, preventing future sagging or leaks that can damage cabinets and subfloors over time.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Janesville, WI

    The most critical part of any sink installation is making sure all connections are leak-proof. Our plumbers use high-quality sealants, proper pipe dope, and correct torque on all fittings to prevent drips and leaks. We conduct thorough water pressure tests after every installation to confirm watertight integrity. This attention to detail protects your cabinets and floors and ensures the life of your new sink in Janesville, WI.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Janesville, WI

    Pedestal sinks are popular for their classic look and space-saving design, but their installation can be tricky due to the exposed plumbing. Our plumbers ensure the drain and supply lines are perfectly aligned within the pedestal column and securely anchored to the wall. This prevents wobbling and ensures all connections hidden and watertight. We handle the delicate nature of ceramic pedestals with care, providing a stable and attractive fixture for your home in Janesville, WI.
