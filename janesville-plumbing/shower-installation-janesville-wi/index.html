    Custom Shower Installation Services in Janesville, WI

    Curbless Shower Installation in Janesville

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproof layers.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Janesville, WI

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are budget-friendly and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and guest houses. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or split.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Janesville

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Janesville, WI

    For new homes and additions, we rough in shower valves, supply lines and drains during framing. Valves are set to depth for the planned wall finish, and drains are located for the chosen pan. We pass rough-in inspections and return to install trim after tile. Our scheduling keeps your build on track.

    Standard Shower Stall Installation in Janesville

    HKV Plumbing installs all types of standard shower stalls, from fiberglass units to acrylic enclosures. We ensure proper leveling, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system. Our team handles the entire process, including securing the base and connecting the drain line, to prevent future leaks and ensure stability. We also verify that water pressure is optimal for your new showerhead.

    Shower Pan Installation in Janesville

    A correctly installed shower pan is fundamental for preventing leaks and protecting your bathroom subfloor. HKV Plumbing carefully installs various shower pan types, including pre-fabricated units and custom-tiled pans with proper waterproofing membranes. We make sure the pan is level and correctly sloped toward the drain, a vital step that prevents costly water damage in the long run. We use high-quality sealants for lasting protection.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Janesville, WI

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most popular bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more accessible shower.
