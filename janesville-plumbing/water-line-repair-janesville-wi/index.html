    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Janesville, WI

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Water softeners are a common feature in many Wisconsin homes, helping protect water heaters, fixtures and appliances from scale. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Janesville, WI

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Janesville

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Janesville

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and longevity, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Janesville, we often see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our experienced plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section. We ensure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Janesville, WI

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Janesville, WI. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We ensure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Janesville

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until significant damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a noticeable drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to accurately pinpoint these hidden leaks in Janesville, reducing the need for extensive excavation. We repair breaks caused by shifting soil, tree root intrusion, or aging pipes, restoring your water service quickly.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Janesville, WI

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.
