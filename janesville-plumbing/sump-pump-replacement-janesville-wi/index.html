    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Janesville

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide a backup. We schedule work to avoid disrupting operations.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Janesville

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Janesville for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and improve efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Janesville, WI

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself may be cracked, damaged or too small. HKV Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Janesville, WI so your entire system functions as it should. A properly sized and intact pit is important for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Janesville, WI

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Janesville, WI, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Janesville

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Janesville, WI

    Zoeller pumps are known for their reliability and heavy-duty construction, making them a top choice for many Janesville, WI homeowners. HKV Plumbing specializes in Zoeller sump pump replacement, so you get a high-quality, long-lasting unit. We can replace your existing Zoeller pump with a comparable model or upgrade you to a more powerful one to meet your needs and local conditions.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Janesville

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Janesville

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle large water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating. This prevents the pump from cycling too often or failing under stress, common issues in some Janesville properties.
