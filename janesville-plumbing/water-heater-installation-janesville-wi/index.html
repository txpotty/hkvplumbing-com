    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Janesville

    The cost of water heater installation in Janesville, WI typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    The average lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all affect the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and make sure the install meets code.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often wise.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.

    HKV Plumbing installs all major reputable brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as many sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring significant plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our first assessment.
